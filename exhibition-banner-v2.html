SQAS is a modern QR-based attendance management system. Teachers generate time-limited codes; students scan from registered phones. GPS geofencing and device binding eliminate proxy attendance.

📊
QR Generation
Time-limited QR codes valid for 15 minutes per class.
📍
GPS Geofencing
Students must be within campus radius (0.1–5 km).
🔒
Device Binding
One student = one phone. Stops proxy attendance.
📈
Live Dashboard
Real-time tracking, 75% rule, CSV export.
